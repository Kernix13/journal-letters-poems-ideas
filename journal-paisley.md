## page 4-6

Fri 9/8/89 11:37 AM

I've been up for nearly 2 hrs - a major accomplishment. Forgot to mention last night the dream I had the night before. I heard it said we only dream in black and white, and rarely in color if at all. Well, since I heard that, I've been keeping awarw of the color of my dreams.

The other night I had a dream with an explosion of color, a big bang in the form of a spectrum. The middle of the dream I was going to meet Tom C. at his swim club. On the path down to the club were houses, rich houses separated by land and trees - beautiful homes.

Where each home met the road, there were many trees on each property. These trees were all blooming with flowers and colors - some were white, some were violet, some were pink, some were yellow and orange and blue and red... So many different colors on each and every property arranged in different patterns - it was beautiful.

In my head I was thinking that these were unique trees that only bloomed close to fall. I was overcome by how so many different ones were all together. I'd love to run into them again when I'm awake in this dream world.

I also forgot to mention the white water rafting trip. On the whole the trip was great. The white water rafting was ok. The greatest thing about the rafting trip was at one point, far down the river, we saw a slow moving freight train crossing a bridge which crossed the water. At the same time we heard a flute sound echoing in the mountains towards our left. We concluded it was from the train. Who knows though. But it was so incredibly beautiful. It sounded like one long note sustained running into a different note and so on. The simplest of songs but "on that note" the most beautiful. I can't describe it any more - it's something I'll never forget. It seemed as if only myself and Mike M. noticed it, or at least paid any attention to it. After rafting, Kevin and I went biking - I gotta get myself a dirt bike
