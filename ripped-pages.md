## 1984 Wed June 6th

Today at practice I got a little disenchanted with the Folcroft swim t eam. I saw that a lot of people really didn't give a damn about working hard, one in particular - Erin McAvey. She could be great if she only worked hard.

Well, because of this and working 5 hrs throughout the day, I felt depressed and confused. I've been wondering why people do the things they do. I don't really answer myself back. I was wondering what was holding Erin back. Paul Rudzitis & Kris, Erin's sister, said it was a lack of responsibility. Anyway, I was a little confused at the end of practice: I didn't know quite how to act, or what to do.

Leaving practice, I happened to glance up at the record board and saw my name in the free relay. I laughed inside that I had happened to catch that one record and wondered, "Would I look up at that record again for some kind of support?" Because it seemed to clear my mind. Next I didn't quite know what the record stood for.

Today I really worked hard, about 5 hrs. In the morning I worked out then went to school for my exam. After that, I lifted and then ran home. Then I had 2 hrs of swimming.

## Sunday June 17th

Tomorrow is swimming trials at Folcroft. I'm pretty excited. I'm glad I'm working out hard, although I could be swimming harder. I'm really hurting for money but then I always am. Everything will turn out though.

## Sat June 23rd

I went up to the mountains and had a great time with PAul Doc and Bob Reid. We got so stomed we couldn't stop laughing. But I know I don't want to do that too much anymore

## Fri June 29th

Tomorrow's a big meet (Tinicum) I pray that we ... but then I'm confident - I hoped do as good in the 50 free as I did last week (32.7, record 34.6) - We Have To Win! We Will Win!

## Tues Aug 28th

Well, I haven't written in a while, so I have a lot to talk about. I don't know where to start because I will probably forget something and then regress. I guess I should start with the biggest event, or rather events, of the summer - that is July 28, my brother's marriage & my championships, first my championships.

I made it in 3 events - I.M., butterfly, and free. I was seeded 5th in the I.M. so I decided to go all out. Well, it payed off in one respect - I moved into 3rd place, but I was also very tired for butterfly which I was seeded 2nd in. Because of being tired I dropped to 4th place, but that was only in my head. FREE - seeded 1st - my best event.

I had been nervous all week because my brother had told me earlier, "If you break the league record that day, it will be the greatest wedding present you could give me. It would add t o the magnificance of the day." Also, at his bachelor's party many of his friends put a case on me, unknowingly of course. They were saying "you have to win for Mike" and "he's been telling us all day how good you are and he expects you to break it" and all that. Well, finally the party ended at Lou Turks, a bar known for its strippers, and there I finally told Turnbridge about all the pressure that was on me. And I'll never forget what he said: "Don't do it for Mike, do it for yourself". And it rang like a bell in my head, "Yeah, I'll do it for myself"

So come Saturday, I was thinking about that record - for myself. But I must admit truthfully that in the back of my head I was saying to myself "you got to do it for Mike, it's his marriage day". And the pressure build until I swam the I.M. - I got tired, didn't have time to think about the record and boom butterflyt and I messed up. I got out, laid down & waited. I walked around and waited. I was up in front of the block - lane 3 - higher than anyone else due to the butterflies. All of a sudden I hear "Do it for yourself". And outloud I said "yeah assholw, do it for yourself, forget all about Mike". But even still I could not stop saying, "But Mike, but Mike..." But if I was wise enough, I would have known that once off the block I would of swam it for myself.

So I'm up on the block, totally wanting the record for myself, calling myself names to make me madder, and all of a sudden "BANG" - we're off. I'm in slow motion, or am I in fast forward? I couldn't tell - the race was over before I noticed but it felt like a thousand life times. I touched the block and stood up, "What was my time?" - "25.5" I hear - "YEAH". I just said to myself, "man, my brother is gonna love this". But I remembered the timers aren't always correct, the true time goes according to the computer. "That god damn computer." I'm pacing around now not sure if I got it or not, when someone, I think Mrs. McAvey, says she'll go up and see what time the computer got me at. "Hurry up please". I see my coach jump into the air and right away I'm a hundred lbs lighter and I know I can breath, and see, and hear. "I got it, it's mine but it's also Mike's"

My time was 25.761 according to the computer. The record was 25.8 so I beat it by 4 100ths of a second. What's even more, the record had been set 18 yrs before which is uncommon.

So know it's a race to get to my brother's wedding on time...

- The most readily identifiable quality of a total winner is an oveerall attitude or personal optimism and enthusiasm.

   - Entertainment
